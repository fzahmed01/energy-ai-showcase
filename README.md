# Energy AI showcase

Small, practical data and AI projects for the clean energy transition, by [Fayaz Ahmed](https://fzahmed01.github.io), an EU Research & Innovation funding consultant and energy engineer.

Each project asks one question that matters for energy communities, flexibility or EU-funded innovation. It answers the question with open data and explains the result in plain language.

| # | Project | Question | Headline |
|:-:|:--|:--|:--|
| 01 | [Community battery flexibility](01-community-battery-flexibility/) | Does running home batteries together beat running them alone? | Shared operation cuts the community's grid peak by 40% |
